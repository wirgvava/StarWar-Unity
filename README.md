Welcome to GalaxyWars – Your Ultimate Space Shooter Experience!

Take command of your spaceship and embark on a high-octane adventure through the cosmos. In GalaxyWars, you’ll test your reflexes and strategy as you aim for the highest score. Destroy menacing monsters, avoid deadly meteors, and collect valuable coins to enhance your gameplay.


### 🕹️ Gameplay Features
- Endless High Score Challenge: Push your limits and keep climbing the leaderboards by continuously improving your high score.<br>
- Intense Battles: Face off against relentless waves of monsters and navigate through treacherous asteroid fields.<br>
- Coin Collection: Gather coins throughout your journey to upgrade your ship and unlock powerful new vessels.<br>
- Thrilling Market: Visit the in-game market to purchase new ships and customize your arsenal to suit your playstyle.<br>
- Global Leaderboard: Compete with players worldwide and see if you can claim a spot in the top 100 high scores.<br>
